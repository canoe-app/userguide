Getting started

  1. Add your class. Tap Add Students and type a name, then press return to add the next one. You can type one name per line to add several at once.
  2. Choose characters. Give each student a character and a card colour.
  3. Mark who is here. Mark students as absent so Random Pick and other tools skip them.
  4. Share it on the big screen. Connect to a TV or projector so the whole class can watch their points grow.

Tip: You can reopen these steps at any time from Settings → Quick Start Guide.

Awarding points

Each student has a card on the Points screen.

  • Tap +1 or +5 to award points.
  • Tap −1 to take a point back.
  • Undo is always at the top right.

If your class uses teams Premium, you can award points to teams and compare their totals.

To reset points, use Reset Points or Reset All Points. Names, jobs and rewards stay, and you can undo the change.

TV and projector

Canoe is made for the whole class to see. Connect your iPad to a projector, TV or display with AirPlay or a cable.

Smart Display

By default, Canoe uses Smart Display. It shows a class-friendly view on the big screen that follows the page or tool you have open, such as the Points Board, Random Pick or the Leaderboard. Settings, editors, Teacher Peek and stats are never shown on the big screen.

Mirror My Screen

To show exactly what's on your iPad instead, go to Settings → Appearance & Accessibility → TV and Projector → Mirror My Screen.

Tip: With Mirror My Screen, the class sees everything you open. Disconnect the display before opening anything private.

Classroom tools

Open the Tools tab and tap any tool. Each one has an ⓘ button that explains how it works.

Show or hide tools in Settings → Classroom Tools. Hiding a tool keeps its data.

Random Pick

  1. Pick a name. Canoe spins through present students and lands on one.
  2. Everyone gets a turn. Nobody is picked twice until everyone has had a turn. Choose Start Over to reset.
  3. Teams too. When your class uses teams, Random Pick chooses a team instead.

Timer

  1. Set the time. Tap the time to choose a duration of up to 3 hours.
  2. Start and carry on. The timer keeps running when you switch tabs or apps. Turn on Show Timer on Points to keep it in view.
  3. Time's up. Canoe plays a chime and shows an alert wherever you are in the app. Turn on notifications to be alerted outside Canoe too.

Group Maker

  1. Choose a mode. Random Groups sorts the class for you. Pick & Choose lets each chosen student pick who joins them.
  2. Set the size. Divide by the number of groups or by students per group. Absent students are left out.
  3. Keep students apart. To stop certain students being placed in the same group, tap the gear icon in the top-left corner and choose Teacher Preferences. These settings are private and never shown to the class.
  4. Ready next time. Canoe remembers the last groups you made. Tap Make New Groups for a fresh mix.

Secret Student Premium

  1. Agree on a goal. Tell the class what you're looking for today, such as kind words, staying on task or lining up quietly.
  2. Canoe picks in secret. Canoe chooses a present student at random. Only you can see who it is, using Teacher Peek. Smart Display never shows it on the TV. If you're mirroring your screen, hide it when you peek.
  3. Keep an eye on them. Give the class reminders through the day, such as "Our Secret Student is having a great morning!"
  4. Goal met: reveal and celebrate. Check Goal achieved and reveal them. Every present student earns +1 point, and the Secret Student earns a +1 bonus.
  5. Goal not met: keep it secret. Choose Goal Not Met. The name stays secret, so nobody is singled out, and no points are awarded.

Mystery Items Premium

  1. Spot the mess. Before tidy-up, look for things that haven't been put away: pencils, wrappers, a stray sock.
  2. Choose them in secret. Add those items in Canoe. If there are two of the same thing, add it twice. Adding a colour or location is optional but helps you tell them apart.
  3. Tidy up together. Tell the class there are mystery items hidden in the mess. Nobody knows which ones count, so everyone pitches in.
  4. Reveal and reward. Reveal the items one at a time and choose who picked up each one. They earn +1 point straight away.

Shared Goals Premium

  1. Set a goal. Choose a reward and a points target, such as 150 points for free choice time. Then choose who's working towards it: students or teams.
  2. Students earn together. Every point a student earns counts towards a student goal, so the class cheers each other on.
  3. Teams work together or race. Team goals use team points. Choose Together to add every team's points to one bar, or Race to give each team its own bar. The first team to reach the target wins.
  4. Show progress. Show goals on the Points screen so everyone can see how close they are.

Rewards Premium

  1. Add rewards. Create rewards such as a sticker, choose a seat or five minutes of free time.
  2. Award them. Give a reward to a student, a team or the whole class. Canoe shows a celebration when one is earned.
  3. Use points for rewards. Turn on Spend Points on Rewards in Settings → Classroom Rules to give rewards a cost.
  4. Check the history. Reward History shows who received what and when, so nobody is missed.

Jobs Premium

  1. Add jobs. Create jobs such as line leader, board monitor or plant carer, and choose how many helpers each one needs.
  2. Assign helpers. Assign students one by one, or choose Assign Helpers to share jobs randomly among students who are present.
  3. Rotate when you like. Assign again whenever it's time for a change, or have Canoe rotate helpers every Monday or every month. Clearing assignments never changes anyone's points.

Leaderboard Premium

Students or teams are ranked on a podium by their current points. Show the leaderboard when it motivates your class. You can hide it in Settings → Classroom Tools.

Points History and Stats Premium

  1. Pick a day. Use the calendar to see every point given on any day.
  2. Look at the bigger picture. Switch to a week, month or school year to see totals, top earners and trends.
  3. Spot who needs a boost. Stats show students with few points, so nobody is overlooked.

Tip: Open Settings → School Year to choose the first and last day of your school year. The School Year view uses these dates.

iCloud Sync and backups

iCloud Sync Premium

Turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings to keep your class the same on your iPad and other devices. Your class is stored in your own private iCloud account and is end-to-end encrypted. The first time you sync, choose whether to keep this device's class or use the class in iCloud.

Backups

Canoe saves automatically. You can also make your own backup with Export Backup and save the file wherever you like.

  • Export Backup is available on every plan.
  • Restore Class from a backup file needs Canoe Premium.

Tip: Backup files aren't encrypted. Keep them somewhere secure and follow your school's privacy rules.

Canoe Free and Canoe Premium

Canoe Free includes

  • Points for up to 12 students
  • Random Pick, Timer and Group Maker
  • Points Board and Smart Display
  • Animal and monster characters
  • Automatic saving and backup export

Canoe Premium adds

  • Whole-class activities: Secret Student, Mystery Items, Shared Goals
  • Rewards and recognition: Rewards, Teams, Leaderboard
  • Classroom management: Jobs, Points History and Stats
  • Students and characters: Up to 100 students, every character collection and every colour theme
  • Sync: iCloud Sync and restoring from a backup

Canoe Premium is available as a monthly or annual subscription, with a one-week free trial for new subscribers. Apple handles all payments.

If Premium ends, all your classroom records stay. Choose which 12 students to use on the Free plan with Choose Free Plan Students, and everything comes back when you resubscribe.

Already subscribed on another device? Tap Restore Purchases.

Help and privacy

Canoe has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. Your classroom information stays on your device unless you choose to turn on iCloud Sync or export a backup. Read the full privacy policy in Settings → Privacy Policy.

Found a bug or have an idea? Use Settings → Send Feedback, or email canoe.app@proton.me.
